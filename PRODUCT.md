# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and authorized operators who need to bill garment printing work. The interface must be usable by someone new to billing software.

## Product Purpose

Thogai lets an operator choose a company, manage its customers, and prepare a bill. Give bill creation and adding a company or customer equal emphasis for first-time users.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This MVP uses browser-local sample data. Company and customer changes stay in the browser. Bills can be previewed and printed as drafts. Keep companies and their customers separate. The final scope describes later document and reporting capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Thogai name. The current redesign replaces the purple palette.

## Evidence on Hand

THOGAI_PROJECT_SCOPE.md describes the product scope. The React app provides the current operational flows.
